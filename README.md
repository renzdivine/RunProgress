# RunProgress

**Your runs, your data, your progress.**

RunProgress connects to your Strava account and transforms your running data into clear, actionable insights — pace trends, distance breakdowns, and progress tracking that Strava doesn't show you.

---

## What It Does

### Connects to Strava
One-click login. RunProgress reads your existing running history — no manual entry needed. Every run you've recorded on Strava automatically appears in your dashboard.

### Tracks Your Progress
See your total runs, total distance, average pace, and average time at a glance. Your entire running history in one place.

### Analyzes Your Pace
Compare runs side by side. See how your 5K tempo pace differs from your long run pace. Understand which workouts build speed and which build endurance.

### Organizes by Distance
Runs are automatically grouped by distance category — 5K, 10K, half marathon, and more. See how you perform across different race distances.

### Works on Your Phone
Install RunProgress as an app on your phone. Works offline — your data is always accessible even without internet.

### Keeps Your Data Safe
Your runs are stored locally and on a secure server. Even if your Strava subscription ends, your existing data stays with you forever.

---

## How It Works

1. **Connect** — Sign in with your Strava account (read-only access, we never modify your Strava data)
2. **Sync** — Your running history imports automatically in seconds
3. **Explore** — View charts, stats, and trends across all your runs
4. **Track** — Add runs manually if needed, delete mistakes, and watch your progress over time

## FAQ

**Do you store my Strava password?**
No. We use Strava's official login — we never see your password.

**What if I cancel my Strava subscription?**
Your already-imported data stays in RunProgress. You can still view all your runs and add new ones manually.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Your data is stored on a secure server and is only accessible by you.

**Can I use it on my phone?**
Yes. Open the site in your phone's browser and tap "Add to Home Screen" to install it as an app.
